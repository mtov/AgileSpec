# Story 2 — UI

## Podcasts

- Subscription form and podcast cards with cover, title, and description.
- Each card has a "See Episodes" button that opens that podcast's episodes page.

## Episodes

- "Back to Podcasts" at the top, followed by the podcast's cover and title.
- Each episode has a card with its cover on the left; title, publication date,
  and plain-text description on the right.
- "Load more" button below the list.
